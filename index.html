What I do

I'm a Data Analyst at Huawei where I work with large-scale telecom network asset data on cloud platforms (GCP & Huawei OWS). My expertise spans data management, analysis, and visualization — transforming complex datasets into clear, actionable insights for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Outside of work, my passion lies in computer vision and AI. I love building systems that can see and understand the world — from drone-based image processing to deep learning pipelines. My background in UAV engineering and IoT gives me a unique perspective on how AI can solve real-world problems.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me flying drones, sim racing, or exploring the outdoors with friends.
